the API

Everything here answers in JSON. A Listing holds things, each a kind (t1 comment, t2 account, t3 link, t4 message, t5 community) with its data; ids are fullnames such as t3_1z, and a listing pages with after and before.

Apps sign in with OAuth2: register one on your apps page, send people to /api/v1/authorize, and trade the code at /api/v1/access_token. Installed apps use PKCE. Send the token as Authorization: Bearer …; no modhash is needed. An app may make 60 requests a minute for each account it acts for, and the X-Ratelimit-* headers say where it stands.

The same, for tools: the OpenAPI document.
